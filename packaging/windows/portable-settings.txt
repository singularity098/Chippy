Chippy keeps its settings, playlist and window layout in this folder,
because it is beside chippy.exe. That makes this copy portable: move the
whole Chippy folder (to a USB stick, say) and they go with it, and
deleting the folder removes every trace of Chippy.

Delete this chippy-settings folder to keep the settings in your Windows
profile instead (%APPDATA%\chippy), as the installed Chippy does.
